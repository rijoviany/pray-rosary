# Privacy Policy for Rosarium

**Effective Date:** October 30, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 30, 2026  

Thank you for choosing **Rosarium: The Holy Rosary** ("Rosarium", "the App", "we", "our", or "us"). We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a sacred, distraction-free devotional environment. 

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information in strict accordance with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and User Data policies.

---

### 1. No Data Collection or Transmission
**Rosarium is a 100% offline, privacy-first application.**

* We **do not** collect, transmit, sell, rent, monetize, or share any personal identifying information (PII).
* We **do not** access or collect your name, email address, physical location, phone number, contacts, media files, or unique hardware identifiers (such as IMEI or Android Advertising ID).
* The App does not require user accounts, logins, passwords, or authentication of any kind.
* The App does not contain network tracking scripts or analytics engines. In fact, Rosarium does not even request or declare internet access permissions (`android.permission.INTERNET`) in its manifest.

---

### 2. Local Device Storage
All prayer configurations and devotional records are stored strictly on your local device using Android Jetpack DataStore:

* **Preferences:** Selected language, prayer duration speed, sound effect levels, haptic toggle, and text font size.
* **Devotional Progress:** 9-day novena day completion milestones, active rosary decade indexes, and prayer streaks / history heatmap.

**Data Retention & Deletion:**  
This data remains exclusively on your device and is never synchronized to cloud servers or accessible to third parties. You have complete control over this data at all times:
* You can reset your prayer history or novenas directly within the app.
* Clearing the App’s data/cache in your Android system settings (`Settings > Apps > Rosarium > Storage > Clear Storage`) or uninstalling the App permanently deletes all local data immediately.

---

### 3. Device Permissions
Rosarium only requests the absolute minimum system permissions necessary to deliver its physical bead simulation:

* **`android.permission.VIBRATE`**:  
  Used solely to operate the device’s internal vibration motor to provide physical, tactile haptic feedback when pulling beads, when the prayer duration completes, and upon spring bounce impacts. No sensory or vibration data is stored or monitored.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services & Advertisements
* **No Advertisements:** Rosarium is completely ad-free. It integrates no advertising networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or Meta Audience Network).
* **No Third-Party Analytics:** We do not embed analytics SDKs (e.g., Google Analytics for Firebase, Flurry, Mixpanel, or AppsFlyer).
* **No External Cloud Services:** No data is sent to external servers or APIs.

---

### 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)
Rosarium is designed for all audiences, including families and children of all ages seeking Catholic prayer and spiritual reflection. Because the App collects no personal information whatsoever from any user, it is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Google Play’s Families Policy.

---

### 6. Security
Since no data is ever transmitted over the internet or stored on external servers, your spiritual practice and application usage remain completely private and confidential on your personal device.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within the application and published with an updated "Last Updated" date. Because the App operates entirely offline, our fundamental commitment to zero data collection remains permanent.
